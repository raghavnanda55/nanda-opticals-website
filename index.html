About Nanda Opticals

Established in 1974, Nanda Opticals has been serving Sri Ganganagar for over 50 years as one of the city's most trusted names in eye care. Our qualified professionals offer accurate vision screening and personalized guidance to help you find eyewear that suits your prescription, your face and your budget.

From everyday prescription glasses to premium designer frames, contact lenses and hearing aid fitting, we bring together experienced service with a carefully curated collection of brands.
